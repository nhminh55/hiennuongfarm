## Rules
- Homepage uses only a curated shortlist; the archive can be comprehensive.
- Time-specific area, production, revenue, workforce and output figures are NOT evergreen facts. Attribute them to publication + date.
- Prefer Hiền Nương-owned photography. Do not copy/hotlink press images unless usage rights are clear.
- Separate press coverage from awards/official recognition, partnerships and community sources.
